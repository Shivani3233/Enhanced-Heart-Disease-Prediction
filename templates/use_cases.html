Emergency Department Triage

In busy emergency departments, CardioPredict provides rapid cardiac risk scoring from vital signs and lab results. The Youden's J optimized threshold ensures maximum sensitivity — prioritizing not missing any true cardiac events.

Key Benefits

  • High sensitivity threshold minimizes missed cardiac events
  • Complements clinical judgment with data-driven scoring
  • Works even with partial clinical data (graceful degradation)

Why These Techniques Matter

Each algorithm was chosen to address a specific challenge in medical prediction.

Class Imbalance

Heart disease datasets are often imbalanced. EasyEnsemble and IWRF with cost-sensitive weights ensure the minority class (disease) is not overwhelmed by healthy cases.

Feature Noise

Not all 27 engineered features are equally useful. BorutaSHAP and mRMR eliminate noisy features, reducing overfitting while keeping all truly relevant signals.

Hyperparameter Sensitivity

ML models are sensitive to hyperparameters. Bayesian Optimization finds optimal settings 5× faster than grid search with better final performance.

Clinical Trust

Doctors need probabilities they can trust. Probability Calibration ensures a 70% risk score truly corresponds to 70% ground truth — essential for informed consent.

Patient Safety

Missing a heart disease case is life-threatening. Cost-Sensitive Learning and Threshold Optimization prioritize sensitivity over specificity for safer screening.

Model Diversity

Different algorithms capture different patterns. Soft Voting across IWRF, LightGBM, CatBoost, and EasyEnsemble reduces individual model weaknesses.
